Therapy often uses simple exercises. For example, mentally naming a thought: “Right now, I have the thought that I won’t manage.” Or picturing the thought written on a piece of paper floating down a river. This isn’t a trick; it’s a way to see that a thought is just an event in the mind, not an absolute truth."
