This is an American YouTube educational channel. Narration style is calm, dry, slightly cynical. No motivation. No inspiration. Just facts, delivered flat and direct. Sentences are short. Pacing is slow and deliberate. Tone feels like a smart guy who stopped caring about impressing you — but every line lands. Voice is detached, slightly amused, brutally honest. No filler words. No hype. No emotional spikes. Feels like a documentary narrator who already knows how the story ends.
