He looks like someone who stopped trying —
and started understanding. ⸻

Soft, heavy body shape
Slightly unkempt hair
Light stubble
Half-tired eyes
Clothing:
Simple t-shirt (slightly worn)
Loose shorts
Basic sneakers
Details:
Always holding or near food (often pizza)
Relaxed posture
Slight slouch ⸻
He should feel like:
“The guy who looks like he gave up — but actually figured everything out.” ⸻

Low energy + Sharp perception + Brutal honesty
